Who we are

We bring good food and thoughtful service.

We are Aspire Catering & Events Services, a catering and event-support team working across Kenya and beyond. We care for corporate and social gatherings, weddings, ruracio, birthdays, anniversaries, graduation ceremonies and burials.

We prepare local and international dishes, including original recipes, with attention to fresh ingredients, hygiene, taste, aroma and nutritional value for adults and children. We shape our service around your preferences and budget, and our well-mannered team is ready to share helpful information about the dishes we serve.
